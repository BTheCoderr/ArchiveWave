# ArchiveWave — Done-for-you service foundation

Status: Proposed operating model; no verified customers, sales, or revenue.

## Positioning
ArchiveWave helps authorized podcast owners inventory older episodes, identify potentially outdated sponsor and promotional segments, and arrange careful, documented cleanup. It is a managed service supported by internal tools, not a self-service audio-editing SaaS.

## Ideal first customer
Independent podcast networks, production companies, and established creators with a meaningful back catalog, editorial control over their recordings, and a reason to review historical ads. Qualify ownership and distribution rights before handling files.

## Offer to validate
- **Free preliminary RSS metadata audit:** episode inventory and possible promotional language in public descriptions; not proof of embedded advertising or lost revenue.
- **$400 pilot / up to 10 episodes:** scoped manual review, candidate timestamps, agreed edits where authorized, QA, and delivery. Confirm actual labor and feasibility before promising turnaround or unlimited revisions.
- **$600/month ongoing service (hypothesis):** recurring archive monitoring and an explicitly agreed monthly work allowance; define capacity only after pilots.

These are proposed prices, not confirmed demand or guaranteed outcomes.

## Customer journey
1. Prospect submits podcast RSS URL and contact information through an approved intake channel.
2. ArchiveWave verifies authority to audit and edit, scope, relevant sponsor contracts, and permission to process audio.
3. Create episode inventory from RSS; use local tools to transcribe only authorized audio.
4. Human reviews timestamped candidates against episode context and contractual obligations.
5. Send audit and itemized proposed changes; receive written approval for exact edits.
6. Preserve originals; edit working copies; run listening and technical QA.
7. Deliver revised files, timestamps/change log, and instructions for customer-controlled publication.
8. Confirm acceptance and offer a clearly scoped recurring service.

## Non-negotiable constraints
- No edits, publication, or sponsor removal without written customer authorization and contract review.
- Never represent a keyword match as a confirmed ad, expired sponsorship, or lost revenue.
- No customer audio, transcripts, private contact details, or credentials in GitHub or CI.
- Retain originals and provide a rollback path.
- No invented testimonials, metrics, customer logos, partnerships, or results.
- Founder approves pricing, spending, outreach, production changes, and final deliverables.

## MVP exit criteria
- Public RSS feed produces a usable episode inventory.
- One authorized sample can be transcribed locally and audited with timestamped findings.
- Reviewer can mark false positives and export a report.
- Written scope/approval process exists before any audio edits.
- One manually QA'd delivery can be reproduced with a change log.
- Pilot economics and customer feedback are documented truthfully.

## Not in initial MVP
Customer self-service editing, automatic publication, payment subscriptions, mass episode downloading, guaranteed ad detection, automatic removal, or speculative AI features.
