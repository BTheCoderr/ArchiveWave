Privacy information

Last updated November 19, 2026. This page describes the planned beta inquiry process and should be reviewed by the business owner before accepting real submissions.

Information requested

The audit inquiry form asks for your name, work email, podcast name, public RSS URL, relationship to the podcast, approximate archive size, and optional project details. Do not include passwords, confidential contracts, private recordings, or sensitive personal information.

Purpose

We use inquiry details to assess fit and respond to your request. Submitting a form does not authorize audio downloads, editing, or publication.

Hosting

The website uses Netlify hosting and its form-processing service. Form submissions may be stored and processed by Netlify under its applicable terms. We have not independently verified the site's live form settings or retention configuration.

Retention and requests

For inquiries about submitted information, contact Bferrell514@gmail.com. Retention and deletion procedures must be finalized before accepting real submissions. Until then, do not submit real personal information through the beta form.
